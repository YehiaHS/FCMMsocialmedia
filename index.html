07 — REFLECTION
"The biggest takeaway was Ms. Nadia's fearless approach — just press record and figure it out."

Stepping out of my comfort zone to record people face-to-face taught me I'm more capable than I assumed — discipline under discomfort became my biggest growth lever.

A 6-hour Open Day shoot with phone storage running out forced real-time problem-solving — I learned to adapt my workflow on the fly and keep delivering.

Editing the final cut — trending audio, beat-sync, voiceover, captions — was the hardest part. It revealed that crafting a polished edit demands patience I didn't know I had.
