## Recommendations

1. **Cover Slide** — Add the industry entity name and your role title below "Industry Placement Portfolio" (e.g. "FCMM Social Media Team · Video Producer") to satisfy G1 fully on the title slide.
2. **Reflection Depth** — Slide 6 currently has a single quote. Add 2–3 more reflection points to show evolved thinking and decision-making. For example: how your editing workflow improved, how you learned to manage time on location, or how your understanding of social media strategy changed.
3. **Logbook Evidence** — Add a brief mention or visual cue referencing logbook entries (e.g. "As documented in Week 3 logbook…" or a small screenshot). This satisfies Section 4 of the requirements.
4. **Discussion Coverage** — Slide 2 lists a "Discussion Coverage" project card, but there is no dedicated slide with a sample for it (unlike Open Day Video on Slide 3 and TikTok on Slide 4). If footage exists, consider adding it.
